I recently modified my astrophotography setup by adding a DIY power distribution box and making a bunch of software improvements. This is the first time I'm using the new configuration, and there's still a lot to improve.

**Author:** Fernando Schuindt  
**License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  
**Camera:** BCF-1 modified Canon T3i  
**Lens/Telescope:** Askar FRA400 400mm f/5.6 quintuplet apochromat  
**Composition:** 30x Light Frames 15" (7'30" total) ISO 1600, 25x dark, 25x flat, 25x bias  
**Processing:** Stacking with DeepSky Stacker, Photoshop Levels adjustments and Camera Raw Filter  
**Location Name:** Aracaju - Sergipe, Brazil  
**Bortle Scale:** 7  
**Location Aprox. Coordinates:** 10°58'11.2"S 37°02'32.5"W  
**Timestamp (Local Time):** 07-13-2026 from 21:57h to 22:39h  
**Timezone:** UTC-3 (No daylight saving time)  
**Outdoor temp.:** 23° C  
**Outdoor humidity:** 79%  
**Theme:** M22  
**Center (RA, hms):** 18h 36m 38.970s  
**Center (Dec, dms):** -23° 53' 36.869"
